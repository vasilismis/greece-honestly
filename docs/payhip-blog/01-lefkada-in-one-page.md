# Lefkada in one page

If you only read one thing before a trip to Lefkada, make it this.

**You can drive there.** Lefkada is joined to the mainland by a causeway and a floating bridge, so there is no ferry to catch. The nearest airport is Aktion (Preveza), about 20 km away on the mainland, and KTEL buses run from Athens' Kifissos terminal in about five and a quarter hours.

**Two coasts, two moods.** The west coast faces the open sea: white cliffs, bright water, sunsets and, on windy days, big waves. The east coast is calmer and greener, with smaller beaches and boat trips. Plan a west-coast day when the sea is calm and keep the east for the windy ones.

**The beaches people ask about:**

- Porto Katsiki: a long flight of steps down from a clifftop car park. Go early in summer.
- Milos: no road. Walk the path from Agios Nikitas or take the water taxi in season.
- Egremni: the 2015 earthquake destroyed the road and steps, and land access has been uncertain since. See it by boat.

**Three bases:**

- Nydri for boat trips to Meganisi and around Skorpios.
- Vasiliki for windsurfing. The wind usually builds in the afternoon, so mornings suit beginners.
- Agios Nikitas for the west-coast beaches and sunsets.

**Go up the mountain.** Karya is known for its embroidery and Eglouvi for its small lentils. Both are a winding drive from the coast, and both are a different world from the beach resorts.

**Look up in town.** Many houses in Lefkada town have a stone ground floor and a light timber upper floor clad in painted metal, a local way of building to cope with earthquakes. The writer Patrick Lafcadio Hearn was born on the island in 1850; Lafcadio, his middle name, comes from it.

**When:** May, June and September are the sweet spots. August is busiest, especially around the 15th. The town hosts its International Folklore Festival in August.

**Hire a car.** The island is big and hilly, and the best places are far apart.
