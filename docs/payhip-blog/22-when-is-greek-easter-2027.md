# When is Greek Easter in 2027, and what closes?

**Orthodox Easter 2027 falls on Sunday 2 May.** That date moves several other public holidays with it, and it is a week when a lot of Greece closes. Here is what to plan around.

## The Easter dates

- **Clean Monday**, the first day of Lent: **Monday 15 March** (48 days before Easter).
- **Good Friday:** **Friday 30 April**.
- **Easter Sunday:** **2 May**, and **Easter Monday: 3 May**.
- **Whit Monday**, 50 days after Easter: **Monday 21 June**. It is a public-sector holiday, and not every private employer observes it.

## The 1 May question

In 2027, **1 May (Labour Day) falls on Holy Saturday**, the day before Easter. No decision had been announced by November 2026. There is a precedent: in 2024, when 1 May fell in Holy Week, the holiday was moved to 7 May for the public sector. Watch for the announcement before you plan.

## The other fixed holidays in 2027

New Year's Day (Friday 1 January), Epiphany (Wednesday 6 January), Independence Day and the Annunciation (Thursday 25 March), the Dormition (Sunday 15 August), Ochi Day (Thursday 28 October) and Christmas (Saturday 25 and Sunday 26 December).

## Why Greek Easter moves

Greek Easter follows the Orthodox church calendar, so it often falls on a different date from Easter in Western Europe. We worked out the 2027 date and the weekdays ourselves and checked them against published holiday calendars.

## What closes

On public holidays, public offices, KEPs, tax offices and banks are closed, and many shops close too. A public holiday that falls on a Sunday is generally not moved to the Monday.

## Plan around it

- **Paperwork:** do not book an AFM, AMKA or bank appointment in Easter week.
- **Travel:** around 15 August, ferries and islands are at their peak, so book early.
- **Shopping:** stock up before the long weekend.
