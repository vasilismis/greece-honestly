# The 7% tax: the three dates people miss

Greece's 7% flat tax on foreign pensions can cut a retiree's tax bill dramatically. On a €40,000 foreign pension, the normal Greek scale comes to about €8,900 before credits; under the 7% regime it is €2,800.

But the regime runs on a calendar, and missing one date can cost you a whole year, or the regime itself. Here are the three dates that catch people out.

## 1. 2 July: the date that decides your first year

When you move your tax residence to Greece matters.

- Moved **on or before 2 July**: you can join the regime for the year you arrived, or from the next year.
- Moved **after 2 July**: you can only join from the next year.

If you have a choice about your moving date, this is worth knowing before you book the removal van.

## 2. 31 October: the application deadline

You apply by **31 October** of the year you want the regime to start.

This date is new. Before Law 5313/2026, the deadline was 31 March. AADE decision A.1192/2026 moved it to 31 October. Plenty of older articles and forum posts still mention March, so check the date of anything you read.

If documents are missing from your application, you can send them by **30 November**. AADE then decides within 60 days, at the latest by the last working day of December.

## 3. The last working day of December: the payment

This is the date people really miss. Under the 7% regime:

- you pay in **one instalment**, by the last working day of December
- there are **no instalment plans**
- if you do not pay in full, **the regime ends from that year**

So the money needs to be ready in December, every year, for up to 15 years.

## One more date, in case you want to leave

You can withdraw from the regime by 31 October of any year. But once you leave, you can never come back in. Think carefully before you withdraw.

## The short version

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 2 July | Decides whether your arrival year can count |
| 31 October | Application deadline (and the deadline to withdraw) |
| 30 November | Last date for missing documents |
| Last working day of December | Pay the full 7% in one go |

Tax treaties and your old country's rules can change the picture, so speak to an accountant before you rely on any of this.
