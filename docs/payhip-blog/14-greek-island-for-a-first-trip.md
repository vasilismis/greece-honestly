# Which Greek island should I choose for my first trip?

It is the question everyone asks, and the honest answer is: it depends less on the island than on the kind of trip you want. Here is a quick way to decide in five minutes.

## Start with three names

For a first island trip, **Naxos, Crete and Paros** are the easiest recommendations. They are easy to reach, have plenty to do, and suit couples and families alike.

## Then match your mood

- **The famous views:** Santorini, ideally in May or October rather than midsummer.
- **Nightlife:** Mykonos or Ios.
- **Peace and quiet:** Folegandros, Sifnos or Paxos.
- **Families:** Naxos, Paros, Corfu or Kos.
- **Hiking:** Crete, Ikaria, Amorgos or Kefalonia.
- **Only a few days from Athens:** Aegina, Hydra or Poros, one to two hours by boat from Piraeus.
- **No ferry at all:** fly straight into Corfu, Crete, Rhodes, Kos, Zakynthos, Kefalonia or Skiathos.
- **Outside summer:** Crete, Rhodes, Syros or Corfu, which keep living all year.

## Bare and dramatic, or green and gentle?

The **Cyclades** are the postcard: white villages, bare hills and blue sea, and windy in July and August. The **Ionian** islands are green, with softer winds and Venetian towns. That one choice narrows the list by half.

## Pick the month too

May and June are the sweet spot: warm and not yet crowded. September has the warmest sea and calmer days. Around 15 August, a national holiday, everything is at its busiest.

## The mistake to avoid

Trying to see five islands in a week. Two or three islands, with at least three nights each, is how people actually enjoy them. Stay within one island group so the ferries connect easily, and keep your last night near your departure airport, because ferry times change every year and wind can delay them.
