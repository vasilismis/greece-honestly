## Description

Planning a trip to the Ionian islands and tired of glossy lists that all say the same thing? This is a practical, plain-English guide to the six main islands: Lefkada, Corfu, Paxos, Kefalonia, Ithaca and Zakynthos.

Every island gets the same clear chapter: how to get there, when to go, where to stay by type of trip, the beaches as they are today, food worth looking for, and the mistakes visitors make. You can compare islands in minutes and choose the one that fits your holiday, not someone else's. It is written for first-time visitors, families, walkers and anyone who prefers green islands and softer winds.

Lefkada gets the most space. It is the home island of the people behind Greece, Honestly, and the island you can drive to over a floating bridge. You will find the west-coast beaches and how to reach them now, why Egremni is best seen by boat, the calm east coast and the boat trips from Nydri, the afternoon wind in Vasiliki, the mountain villages and the local food.

The guide also includes:

- Three sample itineraries: the Ionian by car, Corfu and Paxos, and Kefalonia, Ithaca and Lefkada
- Practical ferry and car tips, from e-tickets to what to do when the wind blows
- Honest notes on beaches with restrictions, such as Navagio and the turtle-nesting beaches of Zakynthos
- 20 useful Greek phrases with simple pronunciation
- Links to our free articles for more detail

Facts were checked against the sources listed at the back in November 2026. Where something could not be confirmed, we left it out rather than guess. Timetables and beach access change every year, so the guide tells you what to check before you go.
