## Description

Moving to Athens is exciting, and the paperwork can be overwhelming. Much of the official information is in Greek, and almost every step depends on another one. This practical guide puts it all in plain English and in the right order.

Inside you will find:

- **Where to live:** five neighbourhoods newcomers often consider, Koukaki, Pangrati, Kypseli, Glyfada and Kifisia, with who each one suits, what it feels like and what to check before you sign. Plus the renting rules that apply everywhere: the three-year minimum term, the deposit and registering the lease with the tax authority.
- **Getting around:** the metro, tram, buses and suburban railway, how to reach the airport, and the ATH.ENA card, including which version a new resident should get.
- **The offices you will need:** what the tax authority, the KEP citizens' service centres, e-EFKA and the police service for foreigners actually do, and which one to visit for what.
- **Your first week:** a day-by-day checklist, from your tax number and a Greek phone number to your lease, bank account, AMKA and residence registration.
- **Health, holidays and everyday life:** healthcare basics, pharmacies at night, emergency numbers and the 2027 public holidays.
- **20 Greek phrases** for offices, shops and transport.

It is written for people from the EU and from outside it, with clear notes where the rules differ, whether you are retiring, working remotely, studying or joining family in Athens.

Facts were checked against the sources listed in November 2026, and anything we could not confirm was left out rather than guessed. Rules, addresses and opening hours change, so the guide tells you what to check before you go.
