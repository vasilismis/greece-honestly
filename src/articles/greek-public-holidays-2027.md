Greek public holidays affect more than a day off. Public offices, banks and many shops close, and around the big holidays ferries and hotels fill up fast. If you are planning a move, an appointment or a trip, it pays to know the dates.

Some Greek holidays have fixed dates. Others move with **Orthodox Easter**, which in 2027 falls on **Sunday 2 May**.

:::note Two dates to double-check
- **1 May 2027** falls on Holy Saturday, the day before Easter. No decision had been announced in November 2026. By law, the Minister of Labour can move the 1 May holiday when it falls on a Sunday, in Holy Week or on Easter Monday, so a move is possible. There is a precedent: in 2024, when 1 May fell in Holy Week, the holiday was moved to 7 May for the public sector. Check the Ministry of Labour's announcement.
- **Whit Monday** is a public-sector holiday, and not every private employer observes it.
:::

## Why Easter moves

Greek Easter follows the Orthodox church calendar, so it usually falls on a different date from Easter in Western Europe. Several holidays are fixed in relation to it:

- **Clean Monday** is 48 days before Easter. In 2027: 15 March.
- **Good Friday** and **Easter Monday** sit either side of Easter Sunday. In 2027: 30 April and 3 May.
- **Whit Monday** is 50 days after Easter. In 2027: 21 June.

## Holidays on a Sunday

Several 2027 holidays fall on a Sunday: Easter Sunday, 15 August and 26 December. A public holiday that falls on a Sunday is generally **not moved** to the Monday. 1 May is the exception, as above.

## What closes

On public holidays, expect:

- **Public offices, KEPs and tax offices** to be closed
- **Banks** to be closed
- **Many shops** to be closed

Cafés, tavernas and tourist businesses in holiday areas are often open, especially in summer. If something matters to you, check locally.

## Planning around the holidays

- **Paperwork:** check the calendar before you plan an AFM, AMKA or bank appointment, especially around long weekends.
- **Travel:** around **15 August**, ferries and islands are at their peak. Book early.
- **Shopping:** stock up before long weekends, such as Easter, when many shops close.

## If you are moving to Greece in 2027

Public holidays matter most in your first weeks, when you depend on offices being open. A few practical points:

- **Count working days, not calendar days.** If your visa or permit deadline falls near a holiday, the office may be closed on the days you were counting on.
- **Spring is busy.** Clean Monday (15 March), 25 March and Easter (30 April to 3 May) are close together. If you arrive in spring, plan your AFM, bank and AMKA steps around them.
- **August slows down.** 15 August is the peak of the summer holidays, and many people take time off around it. Allow extra time for anything that needs an appointment.
- **Late October:** 28 October is a public holiday. If you are applying for the 7% pension tax regime, remember that its deadline is 31 October; do not leave it to the last week.

## Local holidays

Towns and islands also celebrate their own **patron saint's day**, and some local holidays apply only in one place. Ask locally if you have an appointment or a trip planned.
