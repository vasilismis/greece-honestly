The Ionian islands sit off the west coast of Greece. Compared with the Cyclades they are green, the winds are softer and many towns still show their Venetian past. If you like trees, shade and gentle evenings, this is a good part of Greece to start with.

The six main islands are very different from each other, though. Some have international airports and some can only be reached by boat. Some are big enough for a week of driving; one is small enough to explore on a scooter. Here is how they compare.

## At a glance

| Island | Getting there | Best for | Car? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Corfu** | Fly straight in | Families, first visits, trips outside summer | Useful to explore; not needed for a beach holiday |
| **Paxos** | Ferry from Corfu or Igoumenitsa (no airport) | Peace and quiet | Optional: the island is small |
| **Lefkada** | Drive over the floating bridge, or fly to Aktion (Preveza) on the mainland | Dramatic beaches, windsurfing, boat trips | Yes |
| **Kefalonia** | Fly straight in | Hiking, a bigger island to explore | Yes |
| **Ithaca** | Ferry from Kefalonia or the mainland (no airport) | A slow, quiet stay | Helpful |
| **Zakynthos** | Fly straight in, or ferry from Kyllini | Easy access, all year round | Useful to explore |

## The islands one by one

### Corfu

The best-known Ionian island and one of the easiest Greek islands to reach, with direct flights. Corfu suits families and first visits, and it is one of the few islands that is good outside summer too. Corfu town, with its Venetian old town, is a highlight. Corfu is big, so a car helps if you want to see more than one area.

### Paxos

Small, green and quiet. Paxos has no airport, so you arrive by ferry from Corfu or from Igoumenitsa on the mainland. That makes it one of the calmest islands in this group, and a classic choice for a peaceful stay. You can manage without a car.

### Lefkada

The island you can drive to, across a floating bridge from the mainland. The nearest airport is Aktion (Preveza), about 20 km away on the mainland. Lefkada has a dramatic west coast with high cliffs and bright water, a calmer east coast with boat trips from Nydri, and Vasiliki in the south, known for windsurfing. You need a car to see it properly. Read our full [Lefkada guide](/articles/lefkada-travel-guide/).

### Kefalonia

The biggest of the six, mountainous and good for hiking. It has an airport with direct flights. Kefalonia is spread out, with long drives between areas, so plan on a car. It also connects by ferry to Ithaca and, in season, to Lefkada.

### Ithaca

Small and rugged, with no airport. The main crossing is from Sami on Kefalonia to Pisaetos on Ithaca, which takes about half an hour; there is also a longer crossing from Astakos on the mainland. Ithaca suits a slow stay of walking, swimming and village life rather than a busy beach holiday. A car or scooter helps, as villages are spread out.

### Zakynthos

Easy to reach, with direct flights and a year-round ferry from Kyllini on the Peloponnese. That makes it a simple choice if you want little travel hassle. A car is useful if you want to explore beyond your resort.

## Beaches: what to expect

- **Lefkada** has the most dramatic beaches of the six, on its exposed west coast. Access to some has been difficult since an earthquake in 2015; see the [Lefkada guide](/articles/lefkada-travel-guide/) for the current picture.
- **Corfu, Kefalonia and Zakynthos** are big islands with many beaches of different kinds. Choose where you stay according to the beaches you want, because driving between areas takes time.
- **Paxos and Ithaca** are small, so you are never far from the sea; ask locally which coves suit the day's wind.

## Best months

The same pattern applies across the Ionian:

- **May–June:** the sweet spot. Warm, green and not yet crowded.
- **July and August:** hot and busy. Around 15 August, a Greek national holiday, is the busiest time of all.
- **September:** warm sea, calmer days.
- **October:** quieter, and the season starts to wind down.
- **November–March:** small islands like Paxos and Ithaca go quiet; Corfu keeps living.

## Combining islands

Two or three Ionian islands make a lovely trip, as long as you keep it simple:

- **Corfu + Paxos:** fly into Corfu and take the ferry to Paxos for the quiet part of your holiday.
- **Kefalonia + Ithaca:** short, frequent crossings between Sami and Pisaetos.
- **Corfu → Lefkada → Kefalonia by car:** our [Ionian by car route](/articles/greek-island-hopping-three-routes/) does exactly this in ten days.

Ferry times change every year, and some links (such as Lefkada to Kefalonia) only run in season and not every day. Always check current schedules for your dates.

## Which one is right for you?

- **First time in Greece, or travelling with children:** Corfu.
- **Peace and quiet:** Paxos or Ithaca.
- **Dramatic beaches and a road trip:** Lefkada.
- **Hiking and a big island to explore:** Kefalonia.
- **The least travel hassle:** Zakynthos, Corfu or Kefalonia, all with direct flights.
